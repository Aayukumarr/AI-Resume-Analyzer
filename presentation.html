Problem Statement & Motivation

The Recruiter's Challenge

  • Recruiters receive 250+ applications per technical role.
  • Manual resume screening is time-consuming (avg. 6–10 minutes per candidate) and inconsistent.
  • High risk of recruiter fatigue and human subjectivity.

The Candidate's Dilemma

  • 75% of resumes are rejected by automated ATS before human review.
  • Applicants receive zero diagnostic feedback on why their profile failed.
  • Missing critical technical keywords prevents qualified candidates from getting interviews.
Need for an objective, real-time pre-screening system Slide 2 of 12

Project Objectives

1. Ingestion & Extraction

Extract clean text from digital PDF resumes using high-speed binary stream parsing via PyMuPDF.

2. Similarity Scoring

Vectorize resumes and job descriptions with TF-IDF (unigrams + bigrams) and compute cosine similarity (0–100%).

3. Gap Analysis

Categorize technical competencies into 5 domains and compute matched vs. missing skills using set theory.

4. Structure Audit & Actionable Advice

Audit presence of mandatory resume sections (Education, Projects, Experience, Skills, Certifications) and provide instant suggestions.

Mathematical Foundation

1. TF-IDF Formulation

Weighs key words while discarding common corpus terms:

TF(t, d) = f(t, d) / Total Words
IDF(t) = ln((1 + N) / (1 + DF(t))) + 1
TF-IDF = TF × IDF

Uses unigrams & bigrams to capture terms like "machine learning".

2. Cosine Similarity & Set Theory

Measures angular distance between document vectors:

Similarity(A, B) = (A · B) / (||A|| × ||B||)
Score % = round(Similarity × 100)

Skill Gap Logic:

Matched = S_resume ∩ S_job
Missing = S_job \ S_resume

Key Application Features

Diagnostic Output

  • Score Metric: Color-coded progress bar (Green $\ge$ 70%, Blue 40-69%, Amber < 40%).
  • Word Count Metric: Flags resumes that are too short or overly verbose.
  • Two-Column Skills Grid: Clearly displays Matched Skills vs. Skills Missing.

Structure & Usability

  • Section Audit: Verifies presence of Education, Experience, Projects, Skills, and Certifications.
  • Extracted Text Drawer: Inspects raw parsed PDF text for transparency.
  • In-Memory Privacy: No candidate resumes or personal data saved to external disks.

Limitations & Future Enhancements

Current Limitations

  • Scanned Image PDFs: Image-only resumes require OCR, which is not yet integrated.
  • Semantic Synonyms: TF-IDF relies on token overlap; does not know "AWS" == "Amazon Cloud".
  • Keyword Stuffing: Pure statistical models can be manipulated by hidden text.

Future Scope

  • Tesseract OCR: Support for scanned images and graphical resumes.
  • Sentence Transformers / LLMs: Deep semantic dense embeddings (BERT / Gemini API).
  • Multi-Format Parsing: Add direct .docx, .rtf, and .tex support.
  • Bulk Screening: Batch upload of 50+ resumes with automated CSV ranking.
